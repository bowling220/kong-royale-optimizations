Final Benchmark Verdict

Verified Wins, Honest Trade-Offs & What's Next

Summary of the completed main vs. optimization suite across startup, 15 gameplay scenes, 720p–4K scaling, water A/B, and 50-player multiplayer.

Verified Wins

Confirmed
  • +5.9% Mean Gameplay FPS (+12.7% Peak) 14 / 15 Scenes

    Mean in-game FPS rose 98.2 → 103.9 FPS across 15 scenes, with +12.7% in Mango Market Interiors (+24.2% 1% low) and +11.3% in Active Combat.

  • 4K GPU-Bound Gain: +8.9% FPS / -9.7% GPU 3840×2160

    At 4K High, average FPS jumped 77.3 → 84.2 FPS and 1% low improved 15.9 → 24.0 FPS (+50.9%) with GPU time dropping 11.79 → 10.65 ms.

  • Water Rendering: -6% to -9.3% GPU Time Zero Regressions

    Underwater (-9.3%), Peel Harbor Reflections (-8.4%), Open Ocean (-8.2%), and The Murk (-6.0%) all cut GPU time and 11–16 draw calls with clean visual parity.

  • 60 FPS Locked + 50-Player Server Rock Solid Pass

    1080p High VSync holds 59.9 FPS; 50 real WebSocket players hold 59.87 Hz with 0 disconnects, and cold-boot JS heap at full streamed dropped 2044 → 1704 MB (-16.6%).

Honest Trade-Offs & Next Priorities

Next Phase
  • Main-Thread CPU Bottleneck at 1080p (Top Priority) Priority #1

    Dense POIs (Interiors, Combat, Canopy, Vegetation) spend 10.7–11.8 ms/frame on JS/Three.js submission. Cutting draw-call overhead is the key to unlocking 120+ FPS at 1080p.

  • Background World Streaming (+32.9% Cold / +29.8% Warm) Regression

    Smaller streaming chunks (CH 24→6) reduced long tasks (110→99) and cold TBT (-7.2%), but lengthened total background stream from 50.9s → 67.6s. Needs re-tuning.

  • POI Traversal 1% Low (-19.5%) & Underwater Floor (~16 FPS) Investigate

    Rapid 5-POI flyover 1% low dipped 43.3 → 34.9 FPS from streaming/LOD spikes, and Underwater still sees periodic spikes pulling 1% low to ~16 FPS on both branches.

  • Open Tracks: PERF-02, PERF-03 & PERF-04 Queued

    HUD interaction query caching, deterministic loot spatial indexing, and separate tree vs. understory foliage splat budgets.
